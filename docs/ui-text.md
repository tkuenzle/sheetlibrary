# UI text

The voice and the words for what people see and hear in Sheet Library: labels, help, notes, page text, and the names screen readers read. Code, specs and docs keep their own words; for example, they say "caret".

For wording, this page wins over the design canvas. The canvas is the source for layout and looks; its text is not kept up to date.

## Voice

- **Plain:** everyday words a musician uses. No editor jargon such as "caret".
- **Short:** labels are a verb and a noun, such as "Add chord". A help line is one instruction, without a period. Only notes and page text are full sentences.
- **Direct:** say what to do. No "we", "please", "you can" or exclamation marks.
- **Calm:** nothing is an error or a warning unless something is lost.
- **Honest:** say what the app does today. "Coming soon" is the only promise.

All text is in sentence case.

## Glossary

| Use        | Not                | For                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| sheet      | song               | What the library holds. "Sheet music" is fine for the kind of thing, as on the home page.                                                |
| section    | verse, block       | A group of lines. Its label names the part, such as "Verse 1" or "Chorus".                                                               |
| lyrics     | words, text        | The text under the chords.                                                                                                               |
| letter     | character          | One spot in the lyrics, where a chord sits.                                                                                              |
| cursor     | caret              | Where typing happens.                                                                                                                    |
| edit       | change             | Working on a section or a chord.                                                                                                         |
| Done       | OK                 | Closes the chord input and keeps the chord.                                                                                              |
| Remove     | Delete             | Takes a chord off its letter. "Delete" is kept for something gone for good, such as a sheet later.                                       |
| Cancel     |                    | Closes the chord input without keeping what was typed. "Stop editing" (Esc) is a different action.                                       |
| sign in    | log in             | Getting into an account, and "sign out" for leaving it. Signing up is the same as signing in.                                            |
| link       | magic link         | What the sign-in email holds.                                                                                                            |
| passkey    | security key       | The way back in without an email.                                                                                                        |
| version    | copy, variant      | A name and a key to play a sheet in, for who or what it's for, such as "Anna's key" in A.                                                |
| collection | album, folder, tag | A named group of sheets, such as "Christmas", without an order. "Remove" takes a sheet out of it; "Delete" is for the collection itself. |

## Names that stay

- **Section toolbar:** "Add chord", "Move section up" and "Move section down". Docked, the move buttons sit next to "Move", and the add button shows "Chord", but its name is still "Add chord". The toolbar is named "Section 2 of 3".
- **Chord input:** the field is named "Chord". When Done is pressed on text that isn't a chord, the note under it says "Type a chord, such as Am or G7." It says what to do, not that something went wrong.
- **Section label:** the active section without one shows "Add label". The label's field is named "Label", and suggests "Intro", "Verse", "Pre-chorus", "Chorus", "Bridge", "Solo" and "Outro".
- **Key button:** beside the title, "Key G" or "Key Em", read as "Key G" or "Key E minor". Its panel is named "Key", in two parts. "Song key" shows the key, with "Guessed from the chords" while none is set, and "Change", named "Change the song key". "Change" turns the part into "Pick the key the song is in. The chords stay as they are.", "Guessed from the chords: G", the keys under "Major" and "Minor", with a dot on the guessed key when it isn't the song key, and "Cancel". Below a line, "Transpose to" holds the keys to move to. The keys are written as chords are, such as "F#" and "Bbm", and read as "F sharp" and "B flat minor"; the cell with two keys is named "F sharp or G flat". The steps are "Down a semitone" and "Up a semitone", with "One semitone" between them. "Back to G" returns to the song key, and is disabled there. While the sheet is shown in another key, "Make A the song key" sits beside it. The button and the marked key show the key the sheet is shown in, and "Song key" still shows the song's own. While transposed, the button is filled and adds the semitones from the song key, the shorter way, such as "Key A · +2" or "Key F# · −1", read as "Key A, 2 semitones up".
- **Capo button:** beside the key button, "Capo" without a capo, and "Capo 3" with one, read as "Capo 3, G shapes". Its list is named "Capo", with "Pick a fret to see the shapes to play", then "No capo" and "Capo 1" to "Capo 9", each with its shapes, such as "G shapes", read as "Capo 3, G shapes" or "Capo 4, F sharp shapes".
- **Top bar:** on the home page and the prototype editor. It starts with "Sheet Library". Signed out, it ends with "Sign in", only the icon on a phone. Signed in, it ends with the email's first letter in a circle, named "Account".
- **Sheet's bar:** on a sheet, the same at every width, without the site's name or the account. On the play view, the back arrow, named "Back", then "View", "Edit" and the menu button "More for this sheet". "Edit" shows its icon and its name, and on a phone only the icon, still named "Edit". In edit mode, "Done" with a check, the save state, the help button "How to edit" and the menu button.
- **Import:** in the top bar of the editor page, "Import" with an icon, and only the icon on a phone. Its name is "Import from Ultimate Guitar", and so is its dialog's, which "From Ultimate Guitar…" under "New sheet" opens too. The field is named "Chords and lyrics", with the help line "Copy the chords and lyrics from the Ultimate Guitar page and paste them here". Below it, "Written for" offers "No capo" and "Capo 1" to "Capo 9"; when a capo line in the text set it, the help line says "Set from the capo line in the text". On the editor page, the note under it says "Importing replaces the sheet on this page. It can’t be undone."; in the library, there is no note. The buttons are "Cancel" and "Import". When the library's import fails, the dialog says "The text is too long to import." or "The sheet couldn’t be imported. Try again."
- **Import from photos:** "From photos…" under "New sheet" opens the dialog "Import from photos", with the help line "Take or pick a photo of each page, in order" and the buttons "Take photo" and "Pick photos". "Take photo" is only there on phones and tablets. The photos are listed as "Page 1", "Page 2" and so on, each with the button "Remove page 1", and the count beside the buttons says "3 of 10 pages". A photo the browser can't open says "This photo can’t be opened. Remove it, or add it as a JPEG." While the photos are sent, it says "Uploading the photos… 60%"; then "Reading the pages…" with the time so far, such as "0:42", and "This can take up to 2 minutes". When the reading fails, the note says "The photos couldn’t be read. Try again." The buttons are "Cancel" and "Import".
- **Sign-in page:** "Sign in with a passkey", then "or", then the field "Email" with the help line "Get a link by email to sign in" and the button "Send link", which reads "Sending…" while it sends. Once sent, the form gives way to "Check your email" and "A link to sign in is on its way to ana@example.com. It works once, for 15 minutes.", with the button "Use another email". The notes under the form say "Sheet Library is invite-only for now.", "Too many sign-in emails. Try again later.", "The email couldn’t be sent. Try again later.", "No passkey was used. Sign in with an email link instead." and "That link has expired or was already used. Send a new one."
- **Shell:** around the library's screens and the account page. Its first link is "Skip to content", shown only while it has the focus. The navigation is named "Main", with "Sheets" and "Collections". On a wide screen it is a rail that starts with "Sheet Library", then the button "New sheet", and lists each collection under "Collections", with its number of sheets, read as "14 sheets" or "1 sheet". The account is its initial in a circle, named "Account": with the word "Account" at the bottom of the rail, and alone at the top right on a phone. On a middle screen the rail shows the icons with their names under them, after the menu button "Open navigation" and a square button named "New sheet". "Open navigation" opens the full rail, with the close button "Close" next to "Sheet Library". "New sheet" opens a menu named "New sheet", with "Write a new sheet", "From photos…" and "From Ultimate Guitar…".
- **Page errors:** in place of a page that can't be loaded, inside the shell or under the top bar. When the server can't be reached, the heading "Can’t reach Sheet Library" and "Check your connection and try again."; otherwise the heading "Something went wrong". Both have the button "Try again". The loading bar has no text.
- **Page not found:** the heading "Page not found", also in the browser tab, and "This address doesn’t lead to a page in Sheet Library.", with the link "Go to your sheets" when signed in and "Go to the home page" when not.
- **Sheets:** the heading "Sheets", also in the browser tab, with no button beside it. "New sheet" is in the rail (see Shell); on a phone it is a square button with a plus, floating above the bar on every screen of the shell, but not over the empty library's cards. Its menu, named "New sheet", has "Write a new sheet", "From photos…" and "From Ultimate Guitar…"; on a phone they show above the button, which turns into "Close". Between the search field and the list, the section "Recent" shows the sheets opened last as cards: the title, the artist and the key, written and read as in the list. The list is the sheet list below, headed "All sheets" with the number of sheets, such as "All sheets 48", on the line of the order control, and hidden with it while searching. Without sheets, the page says "Add your first sheet" and "Pick a way to start. Everything can be edited later.", with three cards: "Write it", "Start with an empty sheet and type the lyrics and chords."; "From photos", "Take or pick a photo of each page. The lyrics and chords are read into a sheet."; and "From Ultimate Guitar", "Paste the chords and lyrics from an Ultimate Guitar page." A new sheet is titled "Untitled". After an email sign-in, the passkey offer says "Add a passkey to sign in on this device without waiting for an email.", with the link "Add passkey" and the close button "Close".
- **Sheet list:** on Sheets and in a collection. The column labels are "Title", "Artist" and "Key"; on a phone, the artist and the key share a line, as "Leonard Cohen · C". The key is written as chords are, such as "F#m", and read as "F sharp minor"; a sheet without chords shows none. A guessed key looks the same as a set one. Above the list, the order control shows the order in use and opens a menu of the same name, with "Title", "Artist", "Last edited" and "Last added", the one in use checked. Title and artist sort A to Z, the dates newest first. While searching, it is hidden. Each row ends with the menu button "More for Stille Nacht", whose menu holds "Choose collections…" and "Delete", and in a collection "Remove from Christmas" before "Delete". "Delete" asks what it asks on the sheet: "Delete “Stille Nacht”?" and "The sheet is gone for good. This can’t be undone.", with "Cancel" and "Delete". When a delete or a remove didn't get through, the row comes back and the note above the list says "“Stille Nacht” couldn’t be deleted. Try again." or "“Stille Nacht” couldn’t be removed from Christmas. Try again."
- **Collections:** the heading "Collections" and the button "New collection" beside it, below it on a phone. Each card shows the collection's name, how many sheets it holds, such as "14 sheets" or "1 sheet", and its first three titles, A to Z, as "Die Könige, Drüü Könige, O Tannenbaum". Without collections, the screen says "No collections yet", with "New collection" below. "New collection" opens the dialog "New collection", which says "Name it for what it holds.", with the field "Name", empty, and "Cancel" and "Save". A name already used gets the note "That name is already used."; when the save didn't get through, "The collection couldn’t be saved. Try again."
- **A collection:** the heading is its name, with "14 sheets", "1 sheet" or "No sheets yet" below, the button "Add sheets" (on the line below on a phone), and the menu button "More for this collection", with "Rename" and "Delete". The list is the sheet list below, whose row menus add "Remove from Christmas". An empty collection says "No sheets in this collection" and "Add sheets here, or from a sheet with “Choose collections…”.", with "Add sheets" below. "Rename" opens "Rename collection", which says what "New collection" says, with the name filled in. Deleting asks "Delete “Christmas”?" and says "Its sheets stay in the library. This can’t be undone.", with "Cancel" and "Delete", and "The collection couldn’t be deleted. Try again." when it didn't get through.
- **Add sheets:** the dialog "Add sheets to “Christmas”" has the field "Search sheets", with the placeholder "Search titles, artists and lyrics", and a tick box for each sheet. A sheet already in the collection is ticked and can't be unticked, and adds "In this collection" after its artist. When nothing matches, it says "No sheets match “Königin”". The buttons are "Cancel" and "Add sheets", which counts the ticked ones, as "Add 1 sheet" or "Add 2 sheets". When the add didn't get through, the note says "The sheets couldn’t be added. Try again."
- **Collection not found:** the heading "Collection not found" and "This collection doesn’t exist, or it belongs to another account."
- **Library search:** below the heading of Sheets, a field named "Search sheets", with the placeholder "Search titles, artists and lyrics". While searching, it says how many sheets match, such as "4 sheets" or "1 sheet", and ends with the button "Clear search". While a search takes more than a moment, the count says "Searching…". Matches are marked in the title and the artist; a sheet that matches only by its lyrics shows the line with the match under its title. When nothing matches, the list gives way to "No sheets match “Königin”", with the search as typed. When a search can't reach the server, the last results stay, with "Couldn’t search. Showing the last results." below the field.
- **Saving a sheet:** in the bar after "Done", "Saved" once everything has stayed saved for a moment, "Saving…" when a save takes longer than a moment, and "Not saved yet. Trying again…" when a save didn't get through. While edits wait for their save, it says nothing. When the sheet was saved in another tab, it says "Not saved", and the note above the sheet says "This sheet changed in another tab. Reload to see it." When the sheet was deleted elsewhere, it says "Not saved" too, and the note says "This sheet was deleted." with the link "Back to library". When leaving edit mode finds edits it can't save, it asks "Leave without the edits?" and says "The latest edits couldn’t be saved. Leaving loses them.", with "Cancel" and "Leave".
- **Sheet details:** the title's field is named "Title" and the artist's "Artist", which an empty artist field also shows as its placeholder. Beside the title, the icon button "Rename" opens the title's field. Without an artist, "Add artist" shows below the title. A cleared title goes back to "Untitled".
- **Sheet's menu:** the button at the end of the sheet's bar is named "More for this sheet", and so is its menu, the same on the play view and in edit mode. It holds "Choose collections…" and "Print", then, after a line, "Delete". The help on a sheet adds "Click the title to rename the sheet", or "Tap the title to rename the sheet" with touch.
- **First time in edit mode:** a note above the title says "Click a section to edit it. Ctrl K or ⌘ K adds a chord at the cursor.", or with touch "Tap a section to edit it. Chord in the toolbar adds a chord at the cursor.", with the buttons "All shortcuts", which opens the help, and "Got it".
- **Deleting a sheet:** the dialog asks "Delete “Amazing Grace”?" and says "The sheet is gone for good. This can’t be undone.", with the buttons "Cancel" and "Delete". When the delete didn't get through, the note in it says "The sheet couldn’t be deleted. Try again."
- **Play view:** a sheet opens to play. The heading is the sheet's title, with the artist below, then the key and capo buttons, named as in the editor, and above a phone's width the collections that hold the sheet, each by its name, in a list named "Collections". The key panel only shows the sheet in another key: it has no "Change" and no "Make A the song key". "View" opens a panel of the same name: "Text size" with "Smaller text", the size in percent, such as "100%", and "Larger text", then the switch "Lyrics only", on while on. While the lyrics show alone, the key and capo buttons are hidden.
- **Choose collections:** the dialog "Choose collections" shows the sheet's title, then a tick box for each collection, named for it, ticked when it holds the sheet. While the list loads, it says "Loading collections…"; without collections, "No collections yet". The buttons are "New collection", which opens the same dialog as on Collections, and "Done". When a tick didn't get through, the note says "The change couldn’t be saved. Try again."; when the list didn't load, "The collections couldn’t be loaded. Close this and try again."
- **Versions:** on the play view, without versions, a "…" beside the song key, named "Song key: more", holds "Save A as a version…", named for the key the sheet is shown in. With versions, the panel's first part is "Versions": "Song key" with its key, and "Guessed from the chords" while none is set, then each version's name and key, the open one marked, and "Save A as a version…" at the end. The open version's "…", named "Anna's key: more", holds "Make Bb Anna's key" while the sheet is shown in another key, then "Rename…" and "Delete…". "Back to A" names the version's key, and the key button counts from it. The key button adds the open version's name, as in "Key A · Anna's key", after the semitones when shown in another key, as in "Key Bb · +1 · Anna's key". On paper, the name follows the artist, as in "John Newton · Anna's key". The dialog "Save as a version" says "Opens “Amazing Grace” in A. Name it for who or what it’s for.", with the field "Name", filled in with "Key A", and "Cancel" and "Save"; "Rename version" says "Name it for who or what it’s for.". When a save didn't get through, the note says "The version couldn’t be saved. Try again." Deleting asks "Delete “Anna's key”?" and says "The sheet stays as it is. This can’t be undone.", with "Cancel" and "Delete", and "The version couldn’t be deleted. Try again." when it didn't get through.
- **Sheet not found:** the heading "Sheet not found" and "This sheet doesn’t exist, or it belongs to another account."
- **Keys:** as printed on the key, with a space between keys pressed together: "Ctrl K", "⌘ K", "Ctrl Shift L", "⌘ ⇧ L", "Enter", "Backspace", "Esc". Help names both "Ctrl K" and "⌘ K", and both "Ctrl Shift L" and "⌘ ⇧ L", instead of guessing the platform.
