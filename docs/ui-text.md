# UI text

The voice and the words for what people see and hear in Sheet Library: labels, help, notes, page text, and the names screen readers read. Code, specs and docs keep their own words; for example, they say "caret".

For wording, this page wins over the design canvas. The canvas is the source for layout and looks; its text is not kept up to date.

## Voice

- **Plain:** everyday words a musician uses. No editor jargon such as "caret".
- **Short:** labels are a verb and a noun, such as "Add chord". A help line is one instruction, without a period. Only notes and page text are full sentences.
- **Direct:** say what to do. No "we", "please", "you can" or exclamation marks.
- **Calm:** nothing is an error or a warning unless something is lost.
- **Honest:** say what the app does today. "Coming soon" is the only promise.

All text is in sentence case.

## Glossary

| Use     | Not          | For                                                                                                |
| ------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| sheet   | song         | What the library holds. "Sheet music" is fine for the kind of thing, as on the home page.          |
| section | verse, block | A group of lines. Its label names the part, such as "Verse 1" or "Chorus".                         |
| lyrics  | words, text  | The text under the chords.                                                                         |
| letter  | character    | One spot in the lyrics, where a chord sits.                                                        |
| cursor  | caret        | Where typing happens.                                                                              |
| edit    | change       | Working on a section or a chord.                                                                   |
| Done    | OK           | Closes the chord input and keeps the chord.                                                        |
| Remove  | Delete       | Takes a chord off its letter. "Delete" is kept for something gone for good, such as a sheet later. |
| Cancel  |              | Closes the chord input without keeping what was typed. "Stop editing" (Esc) is a different action. |
| sign in | log in       | Getting into an account, and "sign out" for leaving it. Signing up is the same as signing in.      |
| link    | magic link   | What the sign-in email holds.                                                                      |
| passkey | security key | The way back in without an email.                                                                  |

## Names that stay

- **Section toolbar:** "Add chord", "Move section up" and "Move section down". Docked, the move buttons sit next to "Move", and the add button shows "Chord", but its name is still "Add chord". The toolbar is named "Section 2 of 3".
- **Chord input:** the field is named "Chord". When Done is pressed on text that isn't a chord, the note under it says "Type a chord, such as Am or G7." It says what to do, not that something went wrong.
- **Section label:** the active section without one shows "Add label". The label's field is named "Label", and suggests "Intro", "Verse", "Pre-chorus", "Chorus", "Bridge", "Solo" and "Outro".
- **Key button:** beside the title, "Key G" or "Key Em", read as "Key G" or "Key E minor". Its panel is named "Key", in two parts. "Song key" shows the key, with "Guessed from the chords" while none is set, and "Change", named "Change the song key". "Change" turns the part into "Pick the key the song is in. The chords stay as they are.", "Guessed from the chords: G", the keys under "Major" and "Minor", with a dot on the guessed key when it isn't the song key, and "Cancel". Below a line, "Transpose to" holds the keys to move to. The keys are written as chords are, such as "F#" and "Bbm", and read as "F sharp" and "B flat minor"; the cell with two keys is named "F sharp or G flat". The steps are "Down a semitone" and "Up a semitone", with "One semitone" between them. "Back to G" returns to the song key, and is disabled there. While the sheet is shown in another key, "Make A the song key" sits beside it. The button and the marked key show the key the sheet is shown in, and "Song key" still shows the song's own. While transposed, the button is filled and adds the semitones from the song key, the shorter way, such as "Key A · +2" or "Key F# · −1", read as "Key A, 2 semitones up".
- **Capo button:** beside the key button, "Capo" without a capo, and "Capo 3" with one, read as "Capo 3, G shapes". Its list is named "Capo", with "Pick a fret to see the shapes to play", then "No capo" and "Capo 1" to "Capo 9", each with its shapes, such as "G shapes", read as "Capo 3, G shapes" or "Capo 4, F sharp shapes".
- **Top bar:** it starts with "Sheet Library", or with "Library" and a back arrow on a sheet. On a sheet, "Play" and "Edit" follow, as one switch named "Play or edit", with the current page marked; on a phone, only their icons show, still named "Play" and "Edit". Signed out, it ends with "Sign in", only the icon on a phone. Signed in, it ends with the email's first letter in a circle, named "Account".
- **Import:** in the top bar of the editor page, "Import" with an icon, and only the icon on a phone. Its name is "Import from Ultimate Guitar", and so is its dialog's, which the library's "From Ultimate Guitar…" opens too. The field is named "Chords and lyrics", with the help line "Copy the chords and lyrics from the Ultimate Guitar page and paste them here". Below it, "Written for" offers "No capo" and "Capo 1" to "Capo 9"; when a capo line in the text set it, the help line says "Set from the capo line in the text". On the editor page, the note under it says "Importing replaces the sheet on this page. It can’t be undone."; in the library, there is no note. The buttons are "Cancel" and "Import". When the library's import fails, the dialog says "The text is too long to import." or "The sheet couldn’t be imported. Try again."
- **Import from photos:** the library's "From photos…" opens the dialog "Import from photos", with the help line "Take or pick a photo of each page, in order" and the buttons "Take photo" and "Pick photos". "Take photo" is only there on phones and tablets. The photos are listed as "Page 1", "Page 2" and so on, each with the button "Remove page 1", and the count beside the buttons says "3 of 10 pages". A photo the browser can't open says "This photo can’t be opened. Remove it, or add it as a JPEG." While the photos are sent, it says "Uploading the photos… 60%"; then "Reading the pages…" with the time so far, such as "0:42", and "This can take up to 2 minutes". When the reading fails, the note says "The photos couldn’t be read. Try again." The buttons are "Cancel" and "Import".
- **Sign-in page:** "Sign in with a passkey", then "or", then the field "Email" with the help line "Get a link by email to sign in" and the button "Send link", which reads "Sending…" while it sends. Once sent, the form gives way to "Check your email" and "A link to sign in is on its way to ana@example.com. It works once, for 15 minutes.", with the button "Use another email". The notes under the form say "Sheet Library is invite-only for now.", "Too many sign-in emails. Try again later.", "The email couldn’t be sent. Try again later.", "No passkey was used. Sign in with an email link instead." and "That link has expired or was already used. Send a new one."
- **Library:** the heading "Library" and the buttons "Import" and "New sheet". "Import" keeps its text on a phone. It opens a menu named "Import", with "From photos…" and "From Ultimate Guitar…". The column labels are "Title", "Artist" and "Edited"; on a phone, the artist and the last edit share a line, as "Leonard Cohen · Today". The last edit says "Today" or "Yesterday", otherwise the day and month, such as "28 Oct", with the year before this year, such as "3 Sep 2025". Without sheets, the page says "No sheets yet", with "Import" and "New sheet" below. A new sheet is titled "Untitled". After an email sign-in, the passkey offer says "Add a passkey to sign in on this device without waiting for an email.", with the link "Add passkey" and the close button "Close".
- **Saving a sheet:** beside the title, "Saved" once everything has stayed saved for a moment, "Saving…" when a save takes longer than a moment, and "Not saved yet. Trying again…" when a save didn't get through. While edits wait for their save, it says nothing. When the sheet was saved in another tab, it says "Not saved", and the note above the sheet says "This sheet changed in another tab. Reload to see it." When the sheet was deleted elsewhere, it says "Not saved" too, and the note says "This sheet was deleted." with the link "Back to library".
- **Sheet details:** the title's field is named "Title" and the artist's "Artist", which an empty artist field also shows as its placeholder. Without an artist, "Add artist" shows below the title. A cleared title goes back to "Untitled".
- **Sheet's menu:** the button beside the title is named "More for this sheet", and so is its menu. It holds "Rename" and "Delete". The help on a sheet adds "Click the title to rename the sheet", or "Tap the title to rename the sheet" with touch.
- **Deleting a sheet:** the dialog asks "Delete “Amazing Grace”?" and says "The sheet is gone for good. This can’t be undone.", with the buttons "Cancel" and "Delete". When the delete didn't get through, the note in it says "The sheet couldn’t be deleted. Try again."
- **Play view:** a sheet opens to play. The heading is the sheet's title, with the artist below and the key and capo buttons after it, named as in the editor. The key panel only shows the sheet in another key: it has no "Change" and no "Make A the song key". In the top bar, the toggle "Lyrics only" comes first, pressed while on, then the icon buttons "Smaller text", "Larger text" and "Print"; on a phone, they are the items of the menu "More", after the capo button, with "Lyrics only" first and checked while on. While the lyrics show alone, the key and capo buttons are hidden, and on a phone the label "Lyrics only" takes their place.
- **Sheet not found:** the heading "Sheet not found" and "This sheet doesn’t exist, or it belongs to another account."
- **Keys:** as printed on the key, with a space between keys pressed together: "Ctrl K", "⌘ K", "Ctrl Shift L", "⌘ ⇧ L", "Enter", "Backspace", "Esc". Help names both "Ctrl K" and "⌘ K", and both "Ctrl Shift L" and "⌘ ⇧ L", instead of guessing the platform.
