# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Shapes for a capo
The shapes for a capo on a fret SHALL be the key that many semitones below the key that sounds, spelled as the keys to move to spell it. Where two keys are listed, it SHALL be F# for major and Ebm for minor, as for a step. The chords SHALL be moved to the shapes as they are moved to any key.

#### Scenario: Capo 3 in Bb
- **WHEN** the shapes for capo 3 on a sheet in Bb with the chords Bb, Eb and F are worked out
- **THEN** they are in G, and the chords are G, C and D

#### Scenario: Shapes on the tie
- **WHEN** the shapes for capo 4 on a sheet in Bb are worked out
- **THEN** they are in F#
